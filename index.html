Company Description

Founded in 2023, BLoSH is a firm at the forefront of algorithmic trading, using advanced, automated systems to provide stability and liquidity to digital currency markets. We are a fast-growing company with a focus on centralized trading spaces in a decentralized market. Although BLoSH is a young firm, we have already developed a strong presence within the crypto markets, trading a significant amount of worldwide volume. Our mission is to further expand that presence through continuous growth and learning.

Our success comes from our talented team. We work in a simple, straightforward structure using cutting-edge technology that allows us to act quickly on new ideas. We learn and adapt swiftly based on real-time market feedback. In this fast-moving environment, creative thinking and taking initiative are key and highly appreciated.

At BLoSH, we are all about giving our team the support they need to grow and help shape the future of the company. Our team's diverse skill set and ability to learn quickly are what make us stand out. We are a dynamic, innovative market making firm, looking for equally passionate people to help us decode complex market puzzles with precision and flair.

Graduate Trading Analyst

Role Description

Although our trading is highly automated, not everything can or should be handled by a machine. To support our trading operations, we are looking for a Graduate Trading Analyst to work closely with both the Trading and Tech teams across a wide range of automated strategies.

In this role, you will help monitor and support the tooling, data flows, and operational processes that sit around our trading systems. You will be involved in spotting important market announcements, preparing the desk for operational changes, and helping automate workflows that improve speed, reliability, and decision-making.

You will contribute to internal APIs, scrapers, and data pipelines that support trading, and you will work on tools used directly by traders in day-to-day operations. This role is well suited to someone who learns quickly, enjoys variety, and is excited to take ownership of practical systems that have direct impact on trading.

Responsibilities

  • Develop Python tools to automate trading workflows and reduce manual work
  • Investigate, maintain, and improve data pipelines that turn trading data into actionable views
  • Build lightweight APIs and internal utilities to standardize access to important data
  • Create and maintain tools used directly by traders to support decision-making
  • Support trading events that require manual coordination, such as new coin listings, migrations, or rebrandings
  • Stay on top of relevant market and operational changes, and help automate alerts and responses for these events
  • Work closely with traders and engineers to iterate quickly on practical solutions
  • Help ensure internal tools are reliable, maintainable, and performant in a production environment

Requirements

  • Strong Python skills
  • Solid SQL skills and experience working with relational databases
  • Experience building data tools, dashboards, scripts, or internal applications
  • Ability to work closely with end users and translate needs into practical solutions
  • Comfortable working in a Linux environment
  • Strong problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and a willingness to take ownership
  • Interest in trading, market structure, or financial systems is a plus
